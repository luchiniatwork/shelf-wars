# Recruitment & Blind-Test Kits — channels, screening, compensation, kit assembly

Load this file when recruiting playtesters, assembling a blind-test kit, or setting up tester tracking. For the wave protocol itself see `session-protocols.md` §5; for the surveys testers receive see `feedback-instruments.md`.

---

## 1. The recruitment ladder (Caputo's People approach)

Move down the ladder as the design matures; each rung answers questions the previous rung can't.

| Rung | Who | What they give you |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Yourself (solo sim) | Functionality, bug-catch before spending social capital |
| 2 | Friends & family — "friendly playtests" | Safety, enthusiasm check, early engagement reads (Caputo) |
| 3 | Local strangers — meetups, FLGS game nights, design guilds — "unfriendly playtests" | Unbiased first impressions; people who don't care about your feelings |
| 4 | Conventions — Unpub events, Protospiel, Metatopia | Volume of strangers, designer peers, concentrated feedback weekends |
| 5 | Target market — kids for kids' games, families for family games, couples for couples' games | The majority of your tests should be here (Caputo) |
| 6 | Repeat group — one group playing 10+ times | Solvability, long-term balance, "played out by game 5" detection; strategy games require this (Caputo) |
| 7 | Experts — mechanics experts (25-year Scrabble players for a word game), theme experts (police officers for a police game) | Depth critique + authenticity/vetting (Caputo) |

## 2. Channel directory

**In-person events:**
- **Unpub** (Unpublished Games Network) — non-profit playtesting network; runs the Unpub Festival and playtesting tracks at PAX Unplugged/East/West, Origins, Gen Con, SDCC (unpub.net).
- **Protospiel** events — designer-run conventions; the governing norm is reciprocity: "participants are expected to give as much time as they take" — run your game, play others' games (documented by Luke Laurie from Protospiel organizers).
- **Metatopia** (Double Exposure, Morristown NJ) — designer-focused convention with scheduled playtest slots.
- **Local guilds & meetups** — designer groups (e.g., SoCalPlaytesting), FLGS playtest nights, board-game Meetup groups; PlaytestNW for the Pacific Northwest.

**Online blind-test pools:**
- **Break My Game** — 501(c)(3) non-profit; 500+ virtual playtesting events/year; daily and weekly Discord events; community of 4,000+ designers and playtesters; free (breakmygame.com).
- ~~**Blind Playtesters.org**~~ — defunct; domain dead. Use Break My Game or the Cardboard Edison directory instead.
- **Protospiel Online** — Discord + virtual tables, continuous events.
- **Virtual Playtesting / Remote Playtesting** groups — large Facebook/Discord communities.
- **Cardboard Edison's playtest-group directory** (cardboardedison.com) — the canonical maintained list of groups and events; check it before recruiting anywhere.

**PnP communities:**
- **BGG** — Work-in-Progress and Playtesting forums/guilds; PnP enthusiasts routinely build and test strangers' games.
- ~~**playtest-coop.com**~~ — defunct; domain dead. BGG's Playtesting forums and Protospiel Online cover the same need.
- Your own mailing list / Discord once you have one — Stonemaier's model is quiz-screened lead testers recruited from its community (~130 paid, credited leads).

## 3. Screening & compensation

**Screen for the skill that matters:** written reporting of what happened, why, and how it felt. Stegmaier selects lead playtesters with a quiz testing exactly this, plus: leads their own group with intuition (avoids wasted playtests, finishes on time), enjoys writing, gives constructive criticism without redesigning.

Mini-screener (adapt freely):
1. "Recall a game session you played recently. Describe one moment that stood out — what happened, why, and how it felt." (grades the what/why/how-felt skill)
2. "Name a game you love and one specific rule or card you think is flawed, and why." (tests specificity over adjectives)
3. Logistics: can you run 3 sessions within 3 weeks? What group size?

**Compensation norms:**
- Stonemaier: paid — tester's choice of store credit or PayPal; all lead testers credited by name.
- Indie norms: finished/KS-edition copies, credit in the rulebook, small payment for lead testers.
- Campaign/legacy games: significantly raise compensation — you're asking for many sessions from the same group (Stegmaier).
- Convention tests: free, but reciprocity (play others' games) is the currency at Protospiel-style events.

## 4. Blind-test kit checklist

### Physical / PnP kit
- [ ] Complete, playable prototype — every component legible without designer explanation
- [ ] Near-final rulebook (the artifact under test) — version-numbered
- [ ] Structured feedback form (Google Forms typical) with a **version field**: player count, play time, rules pain points, likes/dislikes, balance/fun, open notes, follow-up consent, recording consent (if any recording), tester credit opt-in/out
- [ ] Consent & privacy terms written into the brief (see §6) — required before any kit ships
- [ ] Written instructions: "You are testing the rulebook as much as the game. Do not contact the designer mid-game. If stuck: use intuition, note the question, continue playing." (Stegmaier's guidance)
- [ ] Confidentiality expectations in writing
- [ ] For PnP: PDFs in A4 **and** US Letter; a low-ink variant; explicit list of household items testers must supply (dice, pawns, scissors); page count + estimated assembly time in the invite
- [ ] Your tracking spreadsheet (below)

### Digital kit (TTS/Tabletopia/Screentop/Playingcards.io)
- [ ] Tested share link (open it in an incognito window before sending)
- [ ] Zero-install platform default unless physics justify TTS's per-seat cost
- [ ] Same rulebook + form + instructions as physical
- [ ] Remember: digital obscures footprint, fiddliness, and component legibility (Stegmaier) — schedule a physical verification wave before manufacturing

## 5. Outreach & tracking

**Invite message template:**
> Subject: Blind playtest — [Game], a [weight/length/player count] game — 3 sessions in 3 weeks
>
> I'm looking for a lead playtester to run [Game] with your group. You'd receive [kit/PnP + rulebook] by [date], run 3 sessions within 3 weeks, fill a 5-minute survey after each session, and send one written report at the end. Compensation: [amount/copies/credit]. Confidential until announced. Interested? Two quick questions: [screener link].

**Tester tracking sheet columns:**
```
name | email | source (event/community) | kit sent date | version sent |
sessions done / due | survey 1–3 received | final report received | rating avg |
compensation sent | notes (reliability, group size)
```

**Reliability norms:** send an availability check before the full brief (Stegmaier); expect some drop-off per wave and recruit ~20–30% over target [community practice]; pay promptly; thank and credit everyone — tester goodwill is your long-term recruiting asset, and it survives shelved games but not ghosting.

## 6. Consent & privacy

Designer-side confidentiality (NDAs, "don't share the files") protects *you*; this section protects *them*. Collect the minimum data, say what you'll do with it, and get explicit consent for anything beyond filling in a form [community best practice — no canonical hobby source; requirements derive from general privacy norms and, for minors, COPPA/GDPR-style parental-consent rules].

**Kit/brief consent block (adapt freely):**
> By taking part you agree that: (1) session notes and survey answers may be used to develop the game; (2) sessions will/will not be recorded — recording happens only with your prior written OK, and you may withdraw it at any time; (3) your name and email are used only to run this wave and are deleted within [X months] of the wave closing unless you opt in to follow-ups; (4) you will be credited by name only if you opt in; (5) testers under 18 need a parent/guardian's signed consent, and the parent receives this brief.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] **Recording consent** — explicit, per session, revocable; never record kids without parental consent; state where files live and when they're deleted
- [ ] **Personal data minimization** — name + email + survey answers only; no birthdates, no addresses unless shipping compensation
- [ ] **Retention limit** — state it (e.g., delete tester PII 12 months after the wave or at publication, whichever first); honor deletion requests
- [ ] **Minor testers** — kids' games need kids (Caputo): signed parental consent, parent present or supervising the session, no direct 1:1 contact between designer and minor, no recording without parental sign-off, collect no data from the child beyond gameplay notes
- [ ] **Credit opt-in** — "credited by name" (Stegmaier model) is consent, not default; offer anonymous credit
